# Ayesha Chishti

### Senior SDET · Quality Engineering & Test Automation Leadership

Quality engineering and test automation leader with 9+ years defining and driving QA strategy across web, mobile, cloud and distributed systems. I work automation-first and shift-left, architecting scalable, maintainable test frameworks and embedding automated testing at unit, integration, API and UI levels within CI/CD to cut regression overhead and speed up high-quality releases.

ISTQB Advanced Test Manager (CTAL-TM) certified, with strong ownership of release governance, change management, quality gates and QA standards. My AI focus began with 2021 MSc research designing and evaluating golden datasets, and today I work hands-on across AI-assisted testing and LLM evaluation to keep quality engineering modern and efficient.

---

### What I focus on

- **Test strategy & delivery** — end-to-end strategy across web, mobile and backend; risk-based testing and intelligent regression selection; performance and non-functional testing across APIs and microservices.
- **Automation frameworks** — scalable, maintainable architecture spanning unit, integration, API and UI, wired into CI/CD for continuous testing and fast feedback.
- **Governance & process** — QA governance and standards, change management, release controls, quality gates and KPIs (coverage, defect leakage, release confidence), across Agile / Scrum and full SDLC.
- **AI & LLM evaluation** — LLM-as-judge, golden datasets, groundedness and hallucination checks, multilingual and localisation evals, competitor benchmarking.
- **Leadership** — mentoring QA and SDET engineers, championing a quality-first, shift-left culture, and communicating quality strategy, risk and metrics to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
